# Mobile App Publishing - Brief Overview

## Google Play Store (Android)

### Account Setup
- **Cost:** $25 (~₹2,000) one-time
- **Renewal:** No annual fee
- **Approval:** Immediate after payment
- **URL:** https://play.google.com/console/signup

### Requirements
- **Format:** Android App Bundle (.aab)
- **Min SDK:** Android 6.0 (API 23)
- **Target SDK:** Android 14 (API 34)
- **App Icon:** 512x512px PNG
- **Feature Graphic:** 1024x500px
- **Screenshots:** Minimum 2
- **Privacy Policy:** Mandatory URL

### Review Timeline
- First submission: 3-7 days
- Updates: 1-3 days

### Commission
- 15% for first $1M revenue/year
- 30% after $1M revenue/year

### Payment Rules
- Must use Google Play Billing for digital goods >$3
- Exception: One-on-one services (mentorship) exempt

---

## Apple App Store (iOS)

### Account Setup
- **Cost:** $99 (~₹8,000) per year
- **Renewal:** Annual
- **Approval:** 1-2 weeks (organization requires D-U-N-S number)
- **URL:** https://developer.apple.com/programs/

### Requirements
- **Format:** .ipa file
- **Min iOS:** 14.0+
- **App Icon:** 1024x1024px PNG
- **Screenshots:** Required for all device sizes (iPhone 6.7", 6.5", 5.5", iPad 12.9")
- **App Name:** 30 characters max
- **Subtitle:** 30 characters max
- **Keywords:** 100 characters max
- **Privacy Policy:** Mandatory URL

### Review Timeline
- First submission: 24-48 hours (up to 7 days)
- Updates: 24 hours
- Stricter review than Google Play

### Commission
- 15% for developers earning <$1M/year (Small Business Program)
- 30% standard rate

### Payment Rules
- **Must use Apple In-App Purchase** for digital goods (strictly enforced)
- Cannot link to external payment
- Exception: One-on-one services (mentorship) exempt
- Violations result in app rejection/removal

---

## Progressive Web App (PWA)

### Requirements
- manifest.json file
- Service worker
- HTTPS
- Responsive design

### Features
- Installable from browser
- Works offline
- Push notifications supported
- No app store submission needed

### Costs
- **Setup:** ₹0 (if self-implemented)
- **Annual:** ₹0
- **Commission:** 0% (direct payment gateway only)

## Required Legal Documents

### Privacy Policy
**Must Include:**
- What data you collect (phone, name, email, payment info)
- How you use data
- Third-party services used (Razorpay, Firebase, Zoom, etc.)
- Data retention period
- User rights (access, deletion)
- Contact information

**Format:** Publicly accessible webpage

### Data Safety/Privacy Declaration
**Both Stores Require:**
- Data collection details
- Data sharing practices
- Security measures
- User data deletion process

### Content Rating
**Google Play:**
- Fill questionnaire (5 minutes)
- Typical rating: Everyone or Teen

**Apple App Store:**
- Fill questionnaire
- Typical rating: 12+ or 17+ (medical content)

## App Store Optimization (ASO)

### Google Play Store
**Key Fields:**
- Title: 30 characters
- Short Description: 80 characters
- Full Description: 4,000 characters
- Promotional Text: Updatable anytime

### Apple App Store
**Key Fields:**
- App Name: 30 characters
- Subtitle: 30 characters
- Keywords: 100 characters (crucial, comma-separated, no spaces)
- Description: 4,000 characters
- Promotional Text: 170 characters (updatable without review)

---

## Submission Process

### Google Play
1. Build APK/AAB file
2. Upload to Play Console
3. Complete store listing
4. Submit for review
5. Wait 3-7 days

### Apple App Store
1. Build IPA file via Xcode
2. Upload to App Store Connect
3. Complete app information
4. Provide test account credentials
5. Submit for review
6. Wait 24-48 hours

---

## Common Rejection Reasons

### Google Play
- Privacy policy missing/incomplete
- Permissions not justified
- Data safety form inaccurate
- Screenshots don't match app
- Payment policy violations

### Apple App Store
- App crashes during review
- Features don't work
- Test account doesn't work
- Privacy policy incomplete
- Payment policy violations
- External payment links (for digital goods)
